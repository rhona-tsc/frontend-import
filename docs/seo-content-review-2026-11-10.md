# Artist and priority county content review — 10 November 2026

## Scope and evidence

Reviewed all 24 published artist pages and nine requested county pages: Surrey, Berkshire, Hertfordshire, Essex, Buckinghamshire, Kent, West Sussex, East Sussex and Oxfordshire. A live initial-HTML crawl returned 200 for all 33, each with one main heading and a unique title. County pages have distinct introductions, FAQs, band cards and venue links. Artist biographies contain roughly 140–280 words of approved copy and existing performance-history links; no invented local performance, review, award or credential has been added.

The original supplied Search Console export was historical organic data, not Ads data: the two agency-brand terms accounted for 262 and 171 clicks respectively; "funk wedding band" had 671 impressions, zero clicks and average position 42.46; "motown wedding band" had 141 impressions, zero clicks and position 37.64; "essex bands for hire" had 89 impressions, one click and position 55.47. Its date range was not established, so it is not used as a current-month comparison.

## Changes

1. All artist meta descriptions now prefer the same approved short summary shown on the artist page, replacing the repetitive "Book [name] ... Explore [style] ..." template. Long summaries are trimmed at a word boundary; a factual booking fallback remains for genuinely missing content. Existing artist-specific genre titles remain intact.
2. Magnolia Sky's blank short description was filled from its published biography: "Magnolia Sky brings festival-style folk and pop to weddings and events, with acoustic guitar, uplifting singalongs and 3- or 4-piece lineups."
3. Gotta Be Garage's blank short description was filled from its published biography: "Gotta Be Garage brings live UK garage, R&B, pop and soul to weddings and events, with two female lead vocalists, an MC and a 6-piece lineup."
4. Added one practical, county-specific planning FAQ to each of the nine priority county pages: Surrey sound-limit information; Berkshire quote inputs; Hertfordshire room changeover; Essex garage/R&B selection; Buckinghamshire marquee setup; Kent venue checks; West Sussex daytime/evening movement; East Sussex curfews; Oxfordshire rural access. Advice asks customers to confirm venue conditions rather than claiming universal restrictions.
5. The shared location data keeps these answers identical in initial HTML and interactive pages. Existing canonicals, schema, real local evidence and enquiry flows are retained. No speculative FAQ rich-result claim or artificial rating markup is added.

## Measurement

The existing October 2026 keyword tracker baseline is sparse (one recorded impression across its 24 phrases). Future monthly reports should use unchanged question/keyword sets and show zero-recorded-impression terms with null positions. The watchlist now also covers the nine priority counties and selected artist names. Do not infer success immediately after release: Google can rewrite snippets and recrawling takes time.

AI visibility is tracked in the same monthly digest with separate dated samples, brand mentions and domain citations, once the provider is connected. The two sources measure different things; AI sampling is not traffic, a fixed rank or evidence of a booking conversion. No AI baseline has been fabricated.

## Checks

Public build generates all 51 sitemap pages and verifies visible initial content, schemas and published images. Focused tests cover approved artist descriptions and county data/routes. Existing copy has not been padded for word count, and historical results are not presented as current rankings.
